### My Problem-Solving Methodology



I will follow a consistent problem-solving cycle for every problem.



##### 1\. Understand



\- Read the problem statement carefully.

\- Understand the input, output, constraints, and examples.

\- Identify important observations and edge cases.



##### 2\. Identify Knowledge Gaps



\- Determine which data structures, algorithms, or concepts are required.

\- Study unfamiliar concepts before attempting implementation.

\- Refer to textbooks, documentation, and other learning resources when necessary.



##### 3\. Think Independently



\- Develop a brute-force approach first.

\- Analyze its time and space complexity.

\- Explore possible optimizations.

\- Select appropriate data structures and algorithms.

\- Write the solution logic or pseudocode before coding.



##### 4\. Implement



\- Write the solution independently using the appropriate programming language.

\- Maintain readable code and meaningful variable names.

\- Understand every statement rather than copying existing solutions.



##### 5\. Test



\- Test normal and boundary cases.

\- Consider empty inputs, duplicate values, negative numbers, and other relevant edge cases.

\- Verify correctness and analyze time and space complexity.



##### 6\. Submit



\- Implement the final solution on LeetCode.

\- Submit and verify acceptance.

\- Revisit incorrect submissions to understand the mistakes.



##### 7\. Document



\- Upload the solution to this repository.

\- Organize files according to topics or problem categories.

\- Include the problem name, approach, complexity analysis, and important observations where useful.



##### 8\. Review



\- Revisit previously solved problems.

\- Attempt to reproduce solutions without looking at the original implementation.

\- Review problems after 7 days and 30 days to strengthen long-term retention.
